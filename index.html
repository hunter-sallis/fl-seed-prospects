6 findings · select one to read it
A gap means one of three things: no MLC work carries the hit (57), a work is registered but part of it is unclaimed (40), or the hit’s ISRC is attached to someone else’s song (1). Together these hits carry 321,763,396 measured Spotify plays. 46 of the 98 state Florida in their own bio; the rest are placed in Florida by where their listeners are, which is a weaker signal. Registration status is never payment status — nothing here says what any artist has or has not collected.
